Fruit Punch is an object camp hosted by scorpy, it has pretty low stakes and aims to be a more relaxed camp experience. Challenges will last 1-2 days with emphasis on ideas more than execution, so you don't need to be good at art to do good in general. Fruit Punch aims to be beginner friendly for players to understand parts of camps beyond challenges.
